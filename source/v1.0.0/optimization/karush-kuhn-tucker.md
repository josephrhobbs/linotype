# KKT Conditions

~

::byline[Joseph Hobbs][August 11, 2026]

At the heart of the contemporary theory of optimization is the __Karush-Kuhn-Tucker conditions__ (KKT) for optimality.  The KKT conditions are a collection of first-order conditions necessary (but not sufficient!) for local optimality.  With the added _Slater's condition_, the KKT conditions become sufficient for local optimality, and under the further condition of _convexity_, KKT becomes sufficient for _global_ optimality.

Here, we review the KKT conditions and their connection to Lagrangian duality.
