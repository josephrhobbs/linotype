# Synthesis

~

Many problems in the Information Age revolve around _structured generation_.  Given a collection of game rules and an objective, what is a successful policy that obeys those rules?  Given a high-level program specification (i.e. in the C programming language), what is the optimal sequence of machine instructions to execute the program?  Given a natural language prompt, what is an image which is likely to be found on the Internet under that prompt?

I have decided to collect these various problems and methods under the title __synthesis__, to evoke the formal literature around "program synthesis" and to highlight the "generative" or creative nature of these methods.  Practically, this topic might cover ideas as diverse as reinforcement learning, model-guided and type-directed program synthesis, Markov chain-Monte Carlo samplers, or ODEs and SDEs for denoising ("diffusion", "flow matching", or "score matching") models.

Some _Linotype_ readers may wonder why these ideas were not collected under other topics.  I would like to highlight that, here, the topic of synthesis places a strong emphasis on _structured_ generation... including methods for enforcing specified constraints, whether they are model-based, model-free, or heuristic.
