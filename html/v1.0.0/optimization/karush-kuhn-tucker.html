At the heart of the contemporary theory of optimization is the Karush-Kuhn-Tucker conditions (KKT) for optimality. The KKT conditions are a collection of first-order conditions necessary (but not sufficient!) for local optimality. With the added Slater's condition, the KKT conditions become sufficient for local optimality, and under the further condition of convexity, KKT becomes sufficient for global optimality.

Here, we review the KKT conditions and their connection to Lagrangian duality.
